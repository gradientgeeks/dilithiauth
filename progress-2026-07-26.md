## 6. End-to-End Authentication Workflows Completed

1. **Self-Service Developer Registration**: Sign up with 6-digit OTP email verification via OCI Email Delivery.
2. **OAuth App Management**: Issue Client IDs (`da_...`) and single-view Client Secrets (`dacs_...`).
3. **Backend-for-Frontend (BFF) OAuth 2.0 PKCE Flow**: Secure `HttpOnly` session cookies protecting tokens against XSS.
4. **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)**: NIST FIPS 204 **ML-DSA-44** combined with **Ed25519** hybrid signatures for Jumbo JWTs (~4 KB) and Thin JWTs (~500 B).
